# A note about AI, attribution, and getting things wrong

This project is developed with substantial AI assistance. We learn from existing open-source projects, compare implementations, and build reusable software intended to be freely available to everyone.

Despite our efforts to preserve attribution and respect licenses, we may inadvertently miss a credit, misunderstand a license, or reproduce something we should not have. If we have done so, we sincerely apologize.

Please tell us. Open an issue, point us toward the original work, and feel free to yell at us a little. We'd rather correct a mistake than leave it standing. We'll investigate and make the appropriate correction, whether that means adding attribution, complying with license terms, replacing code, or removing material.

AI assistance is an explanation of our development process, not an exemption from responsibility.

Original software contributions we have the right to license are offered under [MIT](LICENSE). Third-party material retains its applicable terms; see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). This notice is informational, not an additional condition on the MIT grant.

For attribution reports, include the affected path or revision and the original source or rights holder if known. You need not prove a complete licensing case before raising a concern. Please keep credentials and private information out of public issues.
